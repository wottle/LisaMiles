# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-10
First release.

- Two-player (you against the computer) road-race card game for the Lisa Office System 3.0, as an installable tool.
- Full rules: hazards, remedies, safeties, coup fourre, the 700-mile extension (asked only while nobody has extended),
  delayed-action, safe-trip and shutout bonuses, endgame with an empty draw pile, running totals to 5000.
- Click, double-click and drag to play; every legal drop spot lights up, including the discard pile; a dotted outline
  marks the open place in your hand while you draw.
- Original card art drawn as 1-bit icons with captions, and a gauge tool icon.
- Computer opponent with a seeded, reproducible random source.
- Built and tested on LisaEm and a LisaFPGA. Installable from a 400 KB floppy image (tested in LisaEm).
