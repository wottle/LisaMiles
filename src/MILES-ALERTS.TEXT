40 note alert
How to play 1000 Miles^L^L
Be first to travel exactly 1000 miles.  On your turn, click the draw pile, then
click a card in your hand and click where it goes: your side to move or fix
your car, the computer's side to attack it, or the discard pile to throw the
card away.  Or just double-click a card to play it where it belongs.^L^L
You need a Go card to start, and a remedy for every hazard.  Safety cards
protect you for good, and playing one gives you another turn.  Only two
200-mile cards per match.^L^L
Land on exactly 700 miles and you may stop there, or extend to 1000 for a 200
point bonus.  When the draw pile runs out, a player with no card to play is
skipped, and the match ends when neither can play.  The first player whose
score reaches 5000 wins the game.

; -- Extend or stop at exactly 700 miles: OK extends, Cancel stops here.
50 caution ok alert
You have gone exactly 700 miles!^L^L
OK extends the trip to 1000 miles, with a 200 point bonus if you make it.^L
Cancel stops here: the trip is complete and the match ends.
